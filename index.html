ScamFilter —
Fraud Detection

Ethereum wallet risk scoring · part of a larger blockchain analytics platform

A three-layer detection engine for Ethereum wallets, combining blacklist matching against ScamSniffer's scam database with a custom behavioural scoring system.

  • 11-signal heuristic scoring that detects sweeper bots, Sybil attacks and Permit2 drain attacks.
  • Chain-aware signal routing across Ethereum L1 and L2s (Arbitrum, Optimism, Base), with era-specific gas thresholds and ERC-4337 paymaster griefing detection.
  • Automated wallet health classification and a 90-day behavioural recovery system across 100k+ on-chain transaction records.

About Me

I'm a data engineer in Lalitpur, Nepal. Most of my work sits where messy sources meet systems that have to stay up — pipelines, schemas and the monitoring that tells you when something quietly broke.

At Crimson Umbrella Technologies I work on asynchronous ETL, blockchain analytics and the PostgreSQL infrastructure behind them. Before that I spent a summer at Grafi Offshore Nepal on Django and React, learning how features actually get shipped rather than just written.
